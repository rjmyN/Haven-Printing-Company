About Haven Printing

Built on passion, driven by quality — serving Liberia since 2018.

Est. 2018
Our Foundation

A Business Born from
a Vision

Haven Printing Company INC was officially founded on April 24, 2018 in Paynesville, Liberia. What began as a small operation with a big dream has grown into one of the most reliable printing businesses in the greater Monrovia area.

Our founder saw a gap in the local market — businesses and individuals who needed high-quality, affordable printing but had nowhere nearby to turn. Haven was built to fill that gap, and we've been growing ever since.

Today, we serve hundreds of clients — from solo entrepreneurs and community organizations to large companies and government agencies — all with the same warmth and attention to detail we started with.
