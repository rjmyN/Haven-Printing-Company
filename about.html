About Haven Printing

Built on passion, driven by quality — serving Liberia since 2018.

Est. 2018
Our Foundation

A Business Born from
a Vision

Haven Printing Company INC was officially founded on April 24, 2018 in Paynesville, Liberia. What began as a small operation with a big dream has grown into one of the most reliable printing businesses in the greater Monrovia area.

Our founder saw a gap in the local market — businesses and individuals who needed high-quality, affordable printing but had nowhere nearby to turn. Haven was built to fill that gap, and we've been growing ever since.

Today, we serve hundreds of clients — from solo entrepreneurs and community organizations to large companies and government agencies — all with the same warmth and attention to detail we started with.

Meet the Founder & CEO

Founder & CEO
The Visionary Behind Haven

Mr. George Hain Gborgar

Founder & Chief Executive Officer — Haven Printing Company INC

Mr. George Hain Gborgar founded Haven Printing Company INC on April 24, 2018, with a clear vision: to give Liberian businesses and individuals access to world-class printing services right here at home in Paynesville.

With a passion for quality, precision, and community development, Mr. Gborgar has steered Haven from a small startup into one of Liberia's most trusted names in printing. His leadership philosophy is simple — treat every client like family and deliver nothing short of excellence.

"We don't just print — we help businesses communicate their identity. Every banner, every card, every flyer we produce is a reflection of someone's dream. That responsibility drives everything we do at Haven." — Mr. George Hain Gborgar
