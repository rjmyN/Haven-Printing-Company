# Image Sourcing Guide for Haven Printing Company

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Copyright & Legal Issues

**DO NOT use Google Images directly on your website!**

Most images on Google are copyrighted and using them without permission can result in:
- Copyright infringement lawsuits
- Fines from $750 to $30,000+ per image
- Cease and desist orders
- Damage to your business reputation
- Loss of government/NGO contracts

---

## ✅ OPTION 1: Use Your Own Photos (BEST & RECOMMENDED)

### Why Your Own Photos Are Best:
1. **100% legal** - You own the copyright
2. **Authentic** - Shows your real work
3. **Builds trust** - Clients see what you actually produce
4. **Free** - No licensing costs
5. **Unique** - No other company has the same images

### How to Take Good Product Photos:

#### Equipment Needed:
- Your smartphone (iPhone or Android)
- Good lighting (natural light from window is best)
- Clean, neutral background (white paper, wall, or table)

#### Photography Tips:
1. **Lighting:**
   - Take photos during daytime near a window
   - Avoid direct sunlight (causes harsh shadows)
   - Use soft, diffused light (cloudy days are perfect)

2. **Background:**
   - Use clean white paper or cloth
   - Plain wall or table works well
   - Remove clutter from frame

3. **Camera Settings:**
   - Use portrait mode if available
   - Tap to focus on the product
   - Take multiple shots from different angles
   - Use HDR mode for better colors

4. **Composition:**
   - Fill the frame with the product
   - Take straight-on shots and angled shots
   - Include close-ups of details
   - Show product in use when possible

#### What to Photograph:
- [ ] **Business cards** - Stack of 20-30 cards, fanned out
- [ ] **Flyers** - Spread on table, close-up of one
- [ ] **Brochures** - Open and closed views
- [ ] **Banners** - Hanging at events or in your workshop
- [ ] **T-shirts** - Flat lay or on hanger
- [ ] **Stationery** - Letterheads, envelopes arranged nicely
- [ ] **Your workshop** - Printing machines, production area
- [ ] **Events** - Your banners/work at client events
- [ ] **Before/after** - Design mockup vs printed product

### Photo Editing (Optional):
Use free phone apps to enhance photos:
- **Snapseed** (iOS/Android) - Free, professional editing
- **Lightroom Mobile** (iOS/Android) - Free, excellent for color
- **VSCO** (iOS/Android) - Free filters and adjustments

Simple edits:
1. Adjust brightness (+10 to +20)
2. Increase contrast slightly
3. Adjust white balance (make whites pure white)
4. Crop to remove distractions
5. Sharpen slightly

## ✅ OPTION 2: Free Stock Photo Websites (Legal & Safe)

These websites offer **free, legal images** for commercial use:

### 1. **Unsplash** - https://unsplash.com
**License:** Free for commercial use, no attribution required

**Good searches for your business:**
- "business cards printing"
- "banner design"
- "brochure mockup"
- "corporate branding"
- "printing press"
- "t-shirt design"
- "marketing materials"
- "promotional products"

**How to use:**
1. Go to unsplash.com
2. Search for image type
3. Click "Download free" (no account needed)
4. Save to your project folder
5. Rename file (e.g., "banner-sample.jpg")
6. Upload to website

---

**Good searches:**

- "graphic design"
- "paper printing"

**How to use:**
1. Visit pexels.com
2. Search for your topic

```
Subject: Request for Project Photos - Website Portfolio

Dear [Client Name],

We're updating our website and would love to feature the [business cards/banner/brochure] we created for [organization name] in our portfolio.

Would you be willing to share any photos of the finished products? We'd also be happy to photograph them ourselves if that's more convenient.

We'll credit [organization name] as a valued client and it would help showcase the quality of work we deliver.

Thank you for considering this request!

Best regards,
Haven Printing Company INC
+231 779 385 933
```

### What to Ask For:
- Photos of printed products
- Photos from events where your banners/materials were used
- Permission to use their logo on your website
- Testimonial (optional)

### Clients Most Likely to Say Yes:
- Long-term clients
- Recent satisfied customers
- NGOs and businesses (usually more flexible)
- Clients you have good relationships with

## 🚫 NEVER DO THIS

### Illegal & Risky Actions:
❌ Download images from Google Images without checking license
❌ Use competitor's photos
❌ Screenshot images from other websites
❌ Use client logos without written permission
❌ Crop out watermarks from stock photos
❌ Use Pinterest images (most are copyrighted)
❌ Use AI-generated faces for fake testimonials

### Why It's Bad:
- Can be sued for copyright infringement
- Google reverse image search will expose stolen images
- Professional clients will notice
- Damages your credibility
- Can lose your website/domain

## ✅ LEGAL & SAFE PRACTICES

### Always Safe:
✅ Your own photographs
✅ Images from Unsplash/Pexels/Pixabay (check license)
✅ Client-provided photos with permission
✅ Purchased stock photos from Adobe Stock, Shutterstock
✅ Custom graphics you create
✅ Public domain images (very old, no copyright)

### Best Practice Checklist:
- [ ] Read the license terms before downloading
- [ ] Save license information with image
- [ ] Keep record of where each image came from
- [ ] Provide attribution when required
- [ ] Get written permission from clients
- [ ] Never use images with watermarks
- [ ] Check license allows commercial use
