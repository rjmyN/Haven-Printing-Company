Printing & Design

Turnaround time depends on the project:

  • Business cards: 2–3 business days
  • Flyers & brochures: 2–4 business days
  • Banners & large format: 3–5 business days
  • Security printing (ID cards, tickets): 5–7 business days
  • Bulk orders: 7–14 business days

Rush orders are available for an additional fee. Contact us to discuss urgent deadlines.

We accept the following file formats:

  • Preferred: PDF (high resolution, CMYK), AI (Adobe Illustrator), PSD (Photoshop)
  • Accepted: JPG, PNG, EPS, TIFF, CorelDRAW (CDR)
  • Documents: Word, PowerPoint (we'll convert to print-ready format)

Design tip: For best results, provide files at 300 DPI resolution in CMYK color mode with 3mm bleed.

Yes! Our in-house design team can create professional designs for:

  • Business cards, letterheads, envelopes
  • Flyers, brochures, posters
  • Banners, billboards, signage
  • Logos and brand identity
  • Corporate branding materials

Design services are charged separately or included in package deals.

Absolutely! We provide digital proofs for your approval before production begins. For high-value projects, we can also provide physical samples upon request.

Digital printing: Best for small to medium quantities (1–500 units), faster turnaround, cost-effective for short runs. Ideal for business cards, flyers, brochures.

Offset printing: Best for large quantities (500+ units), higher initial setup cost but lower per-unit price. Superior color accuracy and quality for bulk orders.

We'll recommend the best method based on your quantity, budget, and timeline.

We have no minimum order quantity for most products. Whether you need 10 business cards or 10,000 flyers, we can handle it. However, per-unit pricing improves with larger quantities.
