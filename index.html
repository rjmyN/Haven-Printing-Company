Who We Are

Liberia's Trusted
Print Partner

Haven Printing Company INC was founded on April 24, 2018 with one mission: to deliver world-class printing at the heart of Paynesville, Liberia. From a small business card job to a full-scale branding campaign, we treat every order with the same dedication and care.

We combine modern printing technology with a personal touch — because we believe great print work isn't just ink on paper, it's a representation of your brand, your vision, and your reputation.

  • High-quality materials & equipment
  • Fast turnaround times
  • Custom design assistance available
  • Serving all of Monrovia & beyond
