#### 2. **Submit Sitemap to Google**
- Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
- Add your website: `havenprinting.com`
- Verify ownership (multiple methods available)
- Submit `sitemap.xml` under "Sitemaps" section
- Google will start indexing your pages

**Result:** Faster indexing and better rankings.

---

#### 3. **Get Backlinks**
Backlinks = other websites linking to yours. They're like votes for your site.

**Easy ways to get backlinks:**
- List on Liberia business directories
- Get featured in Liberia news sites or blogs
- Partner websites (NGOs, government pages you've worked with)
- Social media profiles (Facebook business page → link to website)
- Local chamber of commerce or business associations
- Supplier and partner websites

**Quality > Quantity** - One link from a government site is better than 100 links from spam sites.

---

#### 4. **Optimize Images**
All your images (logo, business cards, flyers) should:
- Have descriptive file names: `business-cards-haven-printing.jpg` (not `IMG_1234.jpg`)
- Have `alt` text describing the image
- Be compressed (use tools like TinyPNG to reduce file size without losing quality)
- Be in modern formats (WebP is best, but JPG/PNG work fine)

**Why:** Google can't "see" images, but it reads file names and alt text.

---

#### 5. **Create Content Regularly**
Add a simple blog or news section:
- "5 Tips for Designing Business Cards in Liberia"
- "What to Know Before Printing Event Tickets"
- "Haven Printing Partners with [Government Ministry]"
- "New Service: Security Printing Now Available"

**Post 1-2 articles per month** → Google loves fresh content.

## Tracking Your SEO Progress

### Tools to Monitor Rankings:

1. **Google Search Console** (Free)
   - See which keywords bring traffic
   - Track click-through rates
   - Monitor indexing status
   - Find and fix errors

2. **Google Analytics** (Free)
   - Track website visitors
   - See which pages are popular
   - Understand user behavior
   - Track conversions (quote requests, calls)

3. **Google Business Profile Insights** (Free)
   - See how many people find you on Maps
   - Track phone calls and direction requests
   - Monitor photo views

### Key Metrics to Track:
- **Organic Traffic** - Visitors from Google search
- **Keyword Rankings** - Where you appear for important searches
- **Local Pack Ranking** - Position in Google Maps results
- **Bounce Rate** - % of visitors who leave immediately (lower is better)
- **Conversion Rate** - % of visitors who contact you or book

## Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't** keyword stuff (overusing keywords unnaturally)
✅ **Do** write naturally and include keywords where they fit

❌ **Don't** buy backlinks from spam sites
✅ **Do** earn backlinks from quality, relevant sites

❌ **Don't** duplicate content across pages
✅ **Do** write unique content for each page

❌ **Don't** ignore mobile users
✅ **Do** test on mobile devices regularly

❌ **Don't** forget to update Google Business Profile
✅ **Do** keep hours, photos, and info current

❌ **Don't** use generic page titles like "Home" or "Services"
✅ **Do** use descriptive titles: "Professional Printing Services in Paynesville, Liberia"
