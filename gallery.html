Who We Are

Company Overview

Haven Printing Company Incorporated (HPC) is a fully registered Liberian-owned Printing, Branding, Security Printing, and Information and Communication Technology (ICT) solutions company, registered under TIN: 500761801.

Established in April 2018 by our Chief Executive Officer, Mr. George Hain Gborgar, the company has grown into one of Liberia's trusted providers of innovative printing and branding solutions.

Since our establishment, we have successfully partnered with Government institutions, private sector organizations, international and local non-governmental organizations (NGOs), educational institutions, faith-based organizations, and individual clients across Liberia. Our unwavering commitment to quality, professionalism, innovation, and customer satisfaction has earned us a strong reputation and the confidence of our clients.

From humble beginnings in a leased office space, Haven Printing Company has experienced remarkable growth. Within two years of operation, we acquired our own prime property and constructed a modern administrative office while retaining our original facility as a fully operational production workshop. This investment reflects our commitment to long-term sustainability and continuous improvement.

At Haven Printing Company, professionalism, integrity, confidentiality, and timely service delivery are the foundation of everything we do. Over the years, we have successfully executed projects valued at hundreds of thousands of United States dollars for various government ministries, agencies, commissions, and private institutions, many of whom continue to engage our services through long-term business relationships.

Our expertise extends beyond conventional printing. We specialize in high-security printing solutions, including event tickets with anti-counterfeit features, identification cards, certificates, secure documents, and access control systems. We also provide ICT services such as Human Resource Management (HRM) database development, systems integration, data management solutions, and digital security applications.

As technology continues to evolve, Haven Printing Company remains committed to investing in modern equipment, innovative technologies, and skilled professionals to deliver world-class printing, branding, security, and ICT solutions that meet international standards while contributing to Liberia's economic growth and institutional development.

We combine creativity, advanced technology, and professional expertise to provide comprehensive solutions that meet the evolving needs of government institutions, private businesses, international organizations, educational institutions, and individual clients.

As a one-stop printing and ICT solutions center, we offer a wide range of services including graphic design, digital and offset printing, large-format printing, security printing, corporate branding, promotional products, event branding, signage, identification systems, database development, and digital technology solutions. Our integrated approach enables clients to obtain all their printing, branding, and ICT requirements under one roof, ensuring efficiency, consistency, and exceptional quality.

Our Mission

To provide innovative, reliable, and high-quality printing, branding, security printing, and ICT solutions that exceed customer expectations through professionalism, integrity, advanced technology, and exceptional customer service, while contributing to the growth and development of businesses and institutions across Liberia.

Our Vision

To become Liberia's leading printing, branding, security printing, and ICT solutions company, recognized for excellence, innovation, reliability, and customer satisfaction. We aspire to set the benchmark for quality by delivering world-class products, secure technologies, and intelligent business solutions that empower government institutions, corporate organizations, development partners, and individuals.
